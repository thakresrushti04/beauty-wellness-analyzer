# 💄 Beauty & Wellness Industry Analyzer

## Overview
Analyzed 8,000+ Sephora beauty products to identify brand performance, ingredient trends, and customer rating patterns across categories.

## Problem Statement
- Which beauty brands dominate each category?
- What price range gets the highest ratings?
- Which categories have most customer engagement?

## Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle — Sephora Products and Skincare Reviews
- **Products:** 8,216 across 9 categories
- **Price Range:** $3 to $1,900

## Tools & Libraries
`Python` | `Pandas` | `NumPy` | `Plotly` | `Google Colab`

## Key Insights
- **Makeup and Skincare** dominate overall product count.
- **Men's category** has the highest average ratings ($4.62$ at $0-$25 price range).
- **Expensive Makeup ($200+)** underperforms with only $3.0$ average rating.
- **Niacinamide Serum** at $6 has 763k loves — budget products win in engagement.
- **Tools & Brushes** have the most consistent ratings across all price ranges.
